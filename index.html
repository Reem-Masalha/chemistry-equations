COMMON MISTAKES

What to watch for

Changing subscriptsChanging H₂O into H₂O₂ changes the substance. Use coefficients instead.
Forgetting diatomic formsFree oxygen is O₂, not O. The same rule applies to the other six diatomic elements.
Balancing only one elementAlways recount every element after changing a coefficient.
Stopping before reductionA final equation should use the smallest whole-number coefficient ratio.
