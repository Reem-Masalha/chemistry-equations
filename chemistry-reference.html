Introductory solubility rules

  • All Group 1 (alkali metal) and ammonium salts are generally soluble.
  • Nitrates and acetates are generally soluble.
  • Most chlorides are soluble; common exceptions include Ag⁺ and Pb²⁺ salts.
  • Most sulfates are soluble; common exceptions include BaSO₄ and PbSO₄.
  • Most carbonates, phosphates and hydroxides are insoluble unless paired with Group 1 ions or ammonium (hydroxides have additional exceptions).

These are introductory rules, not a complete table. Check your course's full solubility chart for exceptions.
